Summary

Five things to know on day one

Port Vale · 26/27 · after 7 league games

1

Bottom two, but not cut adrift. 23rd on 5 points with two games in hand. Nine points cover 1st to 21st.

2

Home and away are different teams. Unbeaten at Vale Park with one goal conceded. No points and one goal in four away games.

3

We defend well and create too little. xG against 5th best, xG for 22nd. League Two is won on chance quality both ways.

4

Set plays are the quickest fix. Six of our nine goals conceded, nearly three times the play-off rate.

5

The maths from here. 1.82 points a game for the play-offs, 2.00 for automatic, across 39 games.
